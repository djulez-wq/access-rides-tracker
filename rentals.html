Rental Policies

Terms and Conditions
  • Minimum driver age is 19 years.
  • A valid driver's licence and local driving permit where required must be provided.
  • A 25% reservation deposit or full payment is required.
  • Outstanding balances must be paid before vehicle collection.
  • Vehicles must be returned with the same fuel level.
  • Additional drivers require prior approval.
  • Traffic violations are the customer's responsibility.
  • Vehicle damage is subject to the rental agreement and insurance coverage.
  • Additional charges may apply for late returns.
  • Reservations are subject to availability and management approval.
  • All prices are in Eastern Caribbean Dollars (XCD).
Refund and Cancellation Policy
  • Cancellation 7 days or more before pickup: 100% refund of amount paid.
  • Cancellation 3 to 6 days before pickup: 50% refund of amount paid.
  • Cancellation less than 3 days before pickup: no refund.
  • Failure to collect the vehicle: no refund.
  • Cancellation by Access Rides: 100% refund.
  • Early vehicle returns: no refund for unused rental days.
  • Duplicate or incorrect payments will be refunded after verification.
  • Rental date changes are subject to availability.
  • Any price difference must be settled before collection.
  • Approved card refunds will be issued to the original payment method.
  • Refund processing times depend on the bank and customer's financial institution.
  • Cancellation requests must be submitted by email or telephone.
Vehicle Delivery and Collection Policy
  • Airport and hotel delivery are available upon request.
  • Islandwide delivery is available by prior arrangement.
  • Delivery charges depend on location and are confirmed before payment.
  • Vehicles must be collected and returned at the agreed location, date and time.
  • Valid identification, driver's licence and driving permit where applicable are required.
  • Vehicle inspections are conducted before handover and upon return.
  • Customers must notify management of collection delays.
  • Outstanding balances must be settled before vehicle handover.

We collect customer names, telephone numbers, email addresses, rental dates, vehicle preferences and pickup locations to process reservations. Identification and driver's licence information may be required to complete rental agreements.

Customer information is used to manage reservations, confirm payments, arrange delivery, provide customer support and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell customer information. Information may be shared with authorized service providers, payment processors, insurance providers or legal authorities where necessary.

Once online payments are activated, card payments will be processed through an authorized payment gateway.

We take reasonable security measures to protect customer information against unauthorized access, disclosure and misuse.
